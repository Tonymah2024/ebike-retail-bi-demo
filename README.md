# Prairie Pulse E-Bikes — Retail Analytics & BI Demo (VisiVault Analytics)

[![Open in Streamlit](https://static.streamlit.io/badges/streamlit_badge_black_white.svg)](https://prairie-pulse-ebikes-bi.streamlit.app)

**▶ Live dashboard: <https://prairie-pulse-ebikes-bi.streamlit.app>**

Interactive Streamlit dashboard demonstrating retail business intelligence and predictive analytics for a **fictional**
Calgary electric-bicycle retailer, *Prairie Pulse E-Bikes*.

> **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA.** The retailer is fictional. E-bike brands, models and CAD list prices reflect public
> market listings (Oct 2026); Calgary daily weather (Environment and Climate Change Canada) and Bank of Canada indicators
> are real. All sales, cost, inventory, customer, service and expense records are synthetic (seed 20240101).

**Pages:** Executive Overview · Sales Performance · Product & Brand Performance · Profitability · Inventory Intelligence ·
Customer Analytics · Service & Repair Analytics · Sales & Demand Forecasts · Business Scenario Simulator

**Financial Intelligence (pages 10-17):** Income Statement Explorer · Balance Sheet Analysis · Cash Flow Analysis ·
Financial Ratio Dashboard · Working Capital & Inventory Financing · Financial Forecasts · Profitability & Break-even
Simulator · Financial Scenario Comparison.

> **SIMULATED FINANCIAL INFORMATION.** Illustrative branch management accounts generated by double-entry accounting from
> the synthetic operational data (about 19,900 balanced journal entries, monthly trial balances, AR/AP sub-ledgers,
> fixed assets, allocated debt, GST and tax), with an integrated driver-based 12-month forecast (Conservative / Base /
> Optimistic) and sensitivity analysis. All 10 validation checks pass: debits = credits, trial balances reconcile,
> assets = liabilities + equity every month, cash-flow closing cash = balance-sheet cash, inventory and revenue reconcile
> to the operational records, and no balancing plugs. Downloads: `outputs/finance/*.xlsx` (the integrated three-statement
> model uses live formulas and a scenario selector) and `outputs/reports/*.pdf`.

**Behind the dashboard** (pre-computed): validated SQLite model (30 integrity checks), Prophet / SARIMA / XGBoost forecasts with
leakage-safe backtesting, top-down demand forecasts, RFM + K-means segmentation, seasonal reorder-point optimisation with
out-of-sample replay, and a driver-based profit simulator.
